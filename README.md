    <h2>Our Programs & Services</h2>

    <h3><p style="text-align: center;">Youth Empowerment Initiative</p></h3>
    <p><p style="text-align: center;">Description Provides skills development, mentorship, and leadership workshops to equip young people for the future.</p>
    <p><p style="text-align: center;">Target Group: Youth aged 15 to 25.</p>
    <p><p style="text-align: center;">Location: Community Centers across Gauteng and Western Cape.</p>


    <h3><p style="text-align: center;">Community Support & Outreach</p></h3>
    <p><p style="text-align: center;">Description: Offers food security assistance, wellness checks, and companionship programs to support vulnerable residents.</p>
    <p><p style="text-align: center;">Target Group: The elderly and low-income families.</p>
    <p><p style="text-align: center;">Location: Local township centers and mobile outreach units.</p>


    <h3><p style="text-align: center;">Family Unity Workshops</p></h3>
    <p><p style="text-align: center;">Description: Hosts interactive counseling and activity sessions designed to strengthen family bonds and resolve community challenges.</p>
    <p><p style="text-align: center;">Target Group: Families and caregivers.</p>
